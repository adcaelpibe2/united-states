About

This is a gallery of HDR photos showcasing the unique beauty of the U.S. states. HDR photography is a technique that reveals hidden details by merging multiple photos taken at different exposures.

This project was made possible by my interest in exploring and sharing the essence of each place. I’ll update the website as I receive new material. Thanks for visiting!
